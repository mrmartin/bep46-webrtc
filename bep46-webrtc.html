YOU ARE THE INTERNET

Serverless web pages. Addressed by a key, served by whoever has a tab open.

Create new content

Upload a single HTML file. Get a public address you can share and a secret key that lets you publish updates.

Visit a page

Paste a public address. The latest version is fetched from the swarm and rendered here. You silently re-seed it so the page survives the author going offline.

Update your content

Enter your address and secret, pick a new HTML file. The new version is signed and gossiped across the network.
